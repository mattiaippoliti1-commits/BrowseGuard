The derived BrowserGuard dataset is stored in `data/tracker-data.js` and is generated by `scripts/update-tracker-radar.js`.

The generated dataset uses DuckDuckGo Tracker Radar sources for US tracker domains, the generated domain map, and the generated entity map. BrowserGuard transforms and reduces that source material by normalizing tracker categories to BrowserGuard's supported category set, retaining tracking-associated domains in the Analytics, Advertising, and Social categories, excluding unrelated categories such as CDN, Embedded Content, and Other, and compacting the data into domain/category/owner/prevalence entries plus entity-domain mappings.

The currently bundled generated dataset contains 948 tracker domains and 38,370 entity-domain mappings, generated on 2026-09-28 from the upstream `main` source reference.
